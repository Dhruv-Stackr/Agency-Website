# Twenty minutes. Your data. Three gaps.

The first step of every engagement is a free audit call, on your own data. [Pick a time on the calendar]({BOOKING_URL}), or answer the four questions below first: they take a minute, and they are the reason the call is twenty minutes instead of forty.

## Before the calendar.

**1. Your website.**
The address we will look at on the call.

**2. What does "working" look like, in a number?**
Enquiries a month. Demo requests. Orders. How often AI answers name you. If you do not have one yet, write "not sure" and we will shape one together on the call.

**3. Who owns the outcome on your side?**
The person who will open Search Console with us and say yes or no to a fix. If that is you, say so.

**4. How many firms are you talking to?**
An honest answer helps us both. If it is four, we will tell you what we think the odds are and let you decide whether the call is worth your time.

## What happens on the call.

Your words first. Then your Search Console and your ad accounts if you open them, one live AI query for your category, and the source of your own homepage. The three biggest gaps, the uncomfortable one first, each with what it costs you and the fair reason it might exist. What we would do first and what we would measure before the second step. The price, said once. A one-page proposal the next business day.

Nothing on the call touches your accounts. We only look at what you open.
