# Five things we do for your business.

Get you found on Google and in AI answers. Run your Google, Instagram and Facebook ads. Build your website. Run your social media. Make your ads and videos. Each one moves a number you can name, and all five sit in one dashboard you can open any hour.

## We get you found on Google and in AI answers

When someone searches for what you sell, or asks ChatGPT or Google's AI who to go to, your business shows up and gets named. That is what SEO means now, and it is our core work.

**Inside:** fixing whatever stops Google from reading your site · pages that answer the questions your customers actually ask · your Google Business Profile filled in properly, with photos, hours and reviews · getting your name into AI answers, checked seven times a month · for developer tools, docs an AI assistant can read.

**Measured by:** how often you appear, how often you get clicked, and the enquiries that follow, in your own Google Search Console and Analytics. Plus how often AI answers name you, shown as a band.

## We run your Google and Meta ads

Ads on Google search, Instagram and Facebook that bring enquiries this week, at a cost you can see. We fix the tracking first, so every enquiry is traced to the ad that brought it.

**Inside:** Google search ads · Instagram and Facebook ads · the tracking that tells you which ad brought which enquiry · ad creative and testing · budget moved every week toward what is working · a landing page when a campaign needs one.

**Measured by:** cost per enquiry, order or demo, and the enquiries themselves, in your dashboard daily.

**Your spend, your accounts.** You pay Google and Meta directly, from accounts in your own name, at the platform's price.

## We build your website

We design and build your website, or the landing page a campaign needs: fast on a phone, easy to enquire from, and built so Google can read it. You own it from day one.

**Inside:** design · build · fast on a phone · a clear book-now or WhatsApp button on every page · the technical setup search needs, done once, properly · landing pages for campaigns.

**Measured by:** Google's own speed data for your pages, and the enquiries that come through them.

## We run your social media

Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn: posts and reels about what your business is actually doing, on a schedule you can keep, made and posted for you.

**Inside:** a monthly content plan · posts and reels made from your real work · captions in your voice · posting, replies and DMs · the numbers beside everything else in your dashboard.

**Measured by:** the number you chose: enquiries from social, followers who are your buyers, or reach in your city.

## We make your ads, videos and creatives

The pictures, videos and words your ads and posts need, made in-house, so a campaign never waits on a designer.

**Inside:** ad creatives in every size · short videos and reels · offer and festival creatives · a brochure, deck or one-pager when a launch needs it.

**Measured by:** whether it moved the campaign it was made for. Every creative in your dashboard is tagged to the result it produced.

## How the five fit together.

The audit comes first and decides the order. A clinic with broken tracking gets the tracking fixed before a rupee of new ad spend. A shop whose website takes six seconds to open on a phone gets that fixed before anything else. The plan says what runs when, and why, and the dashboard shows it running.
