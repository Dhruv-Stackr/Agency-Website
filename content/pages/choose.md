# How to choose a GEO or SEO agency.

Most "best GEO agency" lists are published by an agency that appears on them, usually first. This page is the other kind. Eight questions to ask, then a list of agencies described by what their own pages say, with the date we checked. Relay is on it, in its own slot, with the same treatment.

## Eight questions to ask before you sign.

**1. "Will you sell me llms.txt, chunking, AI-specific schema or seeded mentions?"**
Google's own guide says you do not need the first three, and that seeking inauthentic mentions is not as helpful as it might seem. An agency that lists any of them as a deliverable is selling you something Google has already said you do not need. Ask, and watch for the pause.

**2. "How many times do you run each prompt before you report a number?"**
One run has a confidence interval of roughly −22% to +122%. Seven is where the standard error drops below 0.10. If the answer is "once" or "the tool handles it", the number is a coin flip.

**3. "Does your AI-visibility tool sample the real interface or an API, and will you put that in the report?"**
Google states that "no third-party tool has access to our internal ranking or AI systems." A share-of-voice number without its sampling method is decoration.

**4. "What is the source under this number?"**
Point at any statistic on their site. A good agency names the study, the sample and the date without looking it up. A number from a tool vendor should be labelled as one.

**5. "Who do you work best with?"**
The best agencies publish an answer. Siege Media states a company fit of 50+ employees or $5 million in revenue or funding. Victorious names a $10 million ARR minimum. An agency that will take anyone has no method that depends on who you are.

**6. "What happens if nothing moves?"**
Listen for a term and an exit. That alone tells you who carries the risk. Month-to-month with a progress clause is the rarer answer.

**7. "Can I see what you did this week without asking?"**
A work log you can open, tied to the numbers, and a written update on a fixed day. Then: owner-level access to GA4, Search Console and the ad accounts, in your own name, from day one. If the accounts are theirs, so is your history.

**8. "Where is the case study expressed in the number I care about?"**
Clients rank conversions first and traffic last among the metrics that matter, and most agency proof is traffic. Ask for one case in enquiries, leads or revenue. If they have none, ask them to say so, and give credit if they do.
