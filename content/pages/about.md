# One person, one team, and a dashboard you can open any hour.

**Dhruv Sharma.** Has spent six years being the first marketing hire in the room, which means six years of being the person who had to find out whether the number was real. Relay is that habit, sold.

The person on the call is the person doing the work. Design, build, campaigns and creative are done in-house with people he has worked with for years; nothing is white-labelled, and every piece of work appears in your dashboard with a name on it.

## Where he has worked.

- **Digistay**: AI software for Indian hotel operators, backed by MeitY. First marketing hire, 2026: paid acquisition, landing pages, the CRM and lifecycle programs, and the reporting the founders ran the business on.
- **Amazon India and Google India**: campaign management and performance reporting at Pulpkey, 2019 to 2022, including a Google India Search launch later referenced inside Google as a success case.
- **Pulpkey**: influencer and social agency, campaign manager and senior reporting analyst.
- **Unhashed**: Web3 go-to-market agency, content lead across several founder-led infrastructure clients.
- **Catalysis**: onchain risk-coverage infrastructure, content and launch communications from zero, on contract.
- **Byzantine Finance**: brand, voice and social, via Unhashed.
- **Lightspeed**: on-ground manager for a Lightspeed hackathon and the online drive for participants, via Crowwd Labs.
- **Camp K12**: edtech, community operations lead, 2021, running the parent and student communities at scale.

## What Relay is.

An agency that does the whole of a small company's marketing, SEO and AI search, paid campaigns, web, social and creative, and shows you all of it. The differentiator is not a service. It is that you can see the work and the numbers any hour, with a source under every number, and that you get a written update every week without asking.

The measurement discipline behind that: [a source under every number](/sources), and a band instead of a score wherever a single run would mislead.

Relay took its current shape on 29 July 2026. There are no case studies yet. The first one will be expressed in the client's number, not in traffic.

## Your accounts stay yours.

Your Google Analytics, your Search Console, your Google Ads, your Meta Ads and your website stay in your name, with you as owner, from day one. We hold the access you grant and nothing more, and we remove ourselves when the engagement ends. If you leave, you leave with everything, and with an export of your dashboard.
