# Privacy.

Short, because there is not much to say.

## What this site collects.

**If you book a call or write to us**, we keep your name, email, phone number, website address and whatever you tell us about your business, in order to prepare for and follow up on the conversation. We keep it for as long as we are talking, and for twelve months after, unless you ask sooner.

**Analytics.** {ANALYTICS_STATEMENT}

**Nothing else.** No advertising pixels, no session recording, no third-party trackers.

## What we do with it.

Prepare for your call. Send you the proposal you asked for. Follow up, at most a few times, and stop when you say stop. We do not sell, rent or share it, and we do not add you to a list you did not ask to join.

## Your rights.

Ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will do so within a week. Write to [{CONTACT_EMAIL}](mailto:{CONTACT_EMAIL}).

We operate from Gurugram, India, and handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework as its rules come into force.
